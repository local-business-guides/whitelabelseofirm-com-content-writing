What press release distribution does

Press release distribution is the process of sharing a prepared announcement through a distribution service or network. It can help put company news in front of publishers and other audiences, but placement and results depend on the distribution option and the announcement itself. It’s not the same as earning editorial coverage, and it shouldn’t be presented to a client as a guarantee of rankings or press mentions.

For an agency, the white label format means you can offer this work under your own brand while a partner handles the service behind the scenes. Set expectations around what is being distributed, where it may appear, and what reporting will be available.

When comparing options, ask how the service is fulfilled, what types of distribution are included, what the dashboard shows, and how client questions are handled. Be wary of promises about guaranteed coverage or search performance. A useful provider should explain the deliverables plainly so you can describe them accurately to your client.

What a campaign can cover

The scope depends on the announcement and the provider’s distribution options. Common planning points include:

  • The announcement’s purpose, intended audience, and final copy.
  • Any distribution categories or reach options available through the provider.
  • Review and approval steps before release.
  • Confirmation or reporting after the announcement is sent.

Timing varies. Allow for writing, client review, edits, and any provider approval before distribution. A straightforward release may move faster than one that needs several rounds of approval, but no fixed turnaround is stated here. Cost also depends on the chosen distribution scope, writing needs, and the work required to prepare the announcement. Ask for a clear outline of what is included before you quote your client.

If you’re comparing providers, look past broad reach claims. Check whether the service supports your client’s actual announcement, what proof of distribution is supplied, and whether the reporting is understandable. Also confirm who is responsible for drafting, revisions, approvals, and client communication.

Press Release Distribution: common questions

Does press release distribution guarantee media coverage?
No. Distribution and editorial coverage are different things, and a provider should not promise that publishers will write about an announcement. Ask what confirmation or reporting comes with the service.
How long does a press release campaign take?
Timing depends on copy preparation, review rounds, approvals, and the selected distribution option. Confirm the expected sequence and timing with the provider before setting a date with your client.
What affects the cost of distribution?
The scope of distribution, writing support, and preparation work can affect the cost. Ask for a description of the included work and any optional services before discussing a client quote.
